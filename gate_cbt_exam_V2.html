⚠ Please read all instructions carefully before clicking "I am ready to begin".

General Instructions

  • Total duration of GATE examination is 180 minutes (3 hours).
  • The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top right of the screen will display the remaining time for you to complete the examination. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself. You do not need to end or submit your examination.
  • The Question Palette displayed on the right side of screen will show the status of each question using one of the following symbols.
Not Visited
Not Answered
Answered
Marked for Review
Answered & Marked

Navigating to a Question

  • To answer a question, do the following: Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly.
  • Click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then go to the next question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to save your answer for the current question, mark it for review, and then go to the next question.

Answering a Question

  • Procedure for answering a Multiple Choice type question: Choose one answer from the 4 options (A, B, C, D) given below the question. Click on the circle button beside the answer.
  • Procedure for answering a Multiple Select type question: Choose one or more answer(s) from the 4 options (A, B, C, D) given below the question. Click on the checkbox button beside each of the selected options.
  • Procedure for answering a Numerical Answer type question: Click on the answer box. A keypad will appear. Use this keypad to enter the answer.
  • To change your answer, click on the option you want to select.
  • To deselect a chosen answer, click on the answer button again or click on the Clear Response button.

Marking Scheme

Question TypeMarksNegative Marking
MCQ – 1 Mark+1–1/3
MCQ – 2 Marks+2–2/3
MSQ – 1/2 Mark+1 or +2No negative
NAT – 1/2 Mark+1 or +2No negative

Sections

  • This paper has 2 sections: General Aptitude (GA) and Subject-specific section.
  • GA has 10 questions (5 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks = 15 marks).
  • Subject section has 55 questions (25 × 1 mark + 30 × 2 marks = 85 marks).
  • Total: 65 questions, 100 marks.
